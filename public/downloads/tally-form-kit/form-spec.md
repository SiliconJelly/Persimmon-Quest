# Persimmon Quest — Start a conversation

Create a single-page contact form for care providers, researchers, collaborators, and investors. No account or email application should be required to submit.

## Fields

1. **Name** — short answer, required
2. **Email** — email field, required, validate email format
3. **Organization** — short answer, optional
4. **Topic** — dropdown, required. Placeholder: Select one
   - Care-facility pilot
   - Research collaboration
   - Product collaboration
   - Investment
   - General inquiry
5. **Message** — long answer, required

## Layout

Place Name and Email in two equal columns. Place Organization and Topic in two equal columns on the next row. Place Message across the full width. Stack all fields into one column on phones.

## Submit

Button label: **Submit**

Show an inline thank-you page after a successful submission:

**Thank you for reaching out**

Your message has been sent to Persimmon Quest. We’ll reply using the email you provided.

## Appearance

Warm ivory background (#fcf9f5), dark ink text (#242522), muted labels (#6e6b66), thin warm borders (#d6cec2), persimmon button (#e8783c). Use a simple sans-serif font, softly rounded fields, and a full-width pill button. Hide the form title in the website embed because the page supplies its own heading.

## Review before publishing

Check required fields, email validation, dropdown choices, two-column layout, mobile stacking, Submit label, and the thank-you page. Tally's file importer generates a draft; verify these settings in its editor.
